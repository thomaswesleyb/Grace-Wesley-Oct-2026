Nearest airports: Chattanooga's airport (CHA) is about 45 minutes from Sewanee, and Nashville's airport (BNA) is about 1 hour 30 minutes away.
Parking: There's plenty of parking around campus. We suggest street parking if it's available, or parking behind Fulford Hall or behind Guerry Auditorium.
Dress code: Formal. Err on the more formal side, but with a touch of whimsy! Dresses should be calf-length to floor-length. Please avoid wearing white, cream, wine red/burgundy, or dark forest green if any of these colors make up more than about 40% of your outfit. White trim, little white leaves, or dark green flowers in a pattern are perfectly fine, and black is absolutely welcome. Gentlemen, get creative too: colored suits, fun ties, and creative pocket squares are encouraged.
Children policy: Yes! Children are welcome.
Welcome statement: We're welcoming guests from many backgrounds and walks of life, and we ask everyone to be kind and respectful to one another.
Registry intro: Our wedding registry is coming soon. Check back here for details.
Things to do nearby: The Princeton Review named Sewanee the most beautiful college campus, so there's lots to see and do outdoors. Hike Shakerag Hollow (medium difficulty), walk from Morgan's Steep to the Cross, or take in Green's View. We love the hike to Bridal Veil Falls, and the Fiery Gizzard trail is a short drive away. There are plenty of great places to camp, and people seem to like the golf course (though we can't vouch for it ourselves). If you're in town Sunday morning, go hear the University Choir. They're good!
Places to eat: Mountain Goat, Fiesta Grill, Stirling's Cafe, Blue Chair Tavern, Shenanigans, and Yamato. For something quick, there's Waffle House, Wendy's, and Taco Bell. Do not, under any circumstances, go to Shan's.
